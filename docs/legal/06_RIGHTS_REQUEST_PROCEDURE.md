# SOMA — Rights Request Procedure v1.0

Date: 2 November 2026
Status: CANONICAL PILOT CANDIDATE

Covers access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and consent withdrawal.

Requests may arrive in-app or by email without legal wording. Track request ID, type, received date, deadline, identity-check status, owner and outcome.

Respond without undue delay and normally within one month. Use proportionate identity verification and do not automatically require identity documents.

Protect third-party data through redaction or separation where feasible. Reporter identity receives enhanced protection.

Provide secure, time-limited exports. Temporary export copies target deletion within 7 days after availability or expiry.

Support rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability where applicable. Dating-consent withdrawal remains a distinct in-app process.

Backups remain isolated. After restore, replay deletion, revocation and restriction before reopening.

Workflow:
RECEIVED → IDENTITY CHECK → SCOPING → DATA COLLECTION → THIRD-PARTY REVIEW → ACTION → RESPONSE → CLOSED

Retain only minimum rights-request evidence, up to 5 years where necessary.

Status: FROZEN / PILOT CANDIDATE
