# SOMA — Age Assurance Assessment v1.0

Date: 2 November 2026
Status: CONDITIONAL PASS

SOMA is strictly 18+.

Pilot model: risk-based / progressive age assurance.

Ordinary onboarding uses a mandatory unpreselected 18+ declaration and does not require universal document or selfie verification.

Layers include:
- age declaration;
- clear 18+ policy;
- report/review path;
- temporary restriction;
- proportionate escalation when reasonable doubt exists;
- challenge/review;
- effectiveness monitoring.

Google auth, QR and university/gym/community membership do not prove age.

Do not use continuous age profiling or internal facial-age estimation in the pilot.

Adding an external age provider requires Provider Matrix, DPIA and Privacy review.

Operational controls are defined by AGE-* in the Legal Control Register.

Status: CONDITIONAL PASS subject to implementation evidence.
