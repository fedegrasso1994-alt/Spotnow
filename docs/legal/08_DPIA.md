## Key decisions
- explicit separate dating consent where required;
- real Non ora path;
- no universal proof-of-age in ordinary onboarding;
- risk-based age review model;
- no behavioral advertising;
- no facial-recognition or biometric-identification feature;
- no attractiveness/desirability score;
- no high-impact fully automated enforcement;
- finite retention;
- provider/transfer review;
- rights workflow;
- backup/restore lifecycle controls;
- incident-response readiness.
