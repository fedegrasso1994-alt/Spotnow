# SOMA — Moderation / Reporting / Appeals Procedure v1.0

Date: 2 November 2026
Status: CANONICAL PILOT CANDIDATE

Maintain distinct handling for ordinary community reports and formal illegal-content notices where applicable.

Use auditable review states and proportionate measures.

Protect reporter identity and avoid creating user-visible side channels through moderation actions.

Removed content should no longer be ordinarily visible and deep/share links should resolve neutrally.

Blocks should be enforced server-side.

Where applicable under the DSA, maintain notice-and-action, statements of reasons and competent-authority escalation procedures.

If micro/small-enterprise exemptions apply, SOMA may maintain a voluntary internal review mechanism without mislabeling it as a mandatory Article 20 system.

Staff moderation access must follow the corresponding Legal Control Register requirements.

Retention follows the Retention Policy.

Status: FROZEN / PILOT CANDIDATE
