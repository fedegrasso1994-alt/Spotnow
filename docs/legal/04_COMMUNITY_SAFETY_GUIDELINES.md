# SOMA — Community & Safety Guidelines v1.0

Date: 2 November 2026
Status: CANONICAL PILOT CANDIDATE

SOMA is 18+ and requires respectful conduct.

Users must not impersonate others, misuse the service to harass or intimidate, publish private identifying information without authorization, abuse reporting tools, bypass blocks, or interfere with service security.

Spotted is anonymous to peers, not to SOMA.

ORA is a declaration of presence, not proof of physical location.

SOMA may apply proportionate measures including warnings, content removal, feature limitations, temporary restrictions, suspension or account closure.

Good-faith reporting is allowed. Where required or appropriate, SOMA should provide reasons and a review path.

Relevant evidence may be retained only as necessary under the Retention Policy.

Operational requirements for moderation and reporting are defined in the Legal Control Register.

Status: FROZEN / PILOT CANDIDATE
