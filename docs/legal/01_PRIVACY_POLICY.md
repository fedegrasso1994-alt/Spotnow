# SOMA — Privacy Policy v1.0 — Pilot Candidate

**Effective date:** 2 November 2026  
**Controller:** Federico Grasso  
**Privacy contact:** somadatingapp@gmail.com

## 1. Scope

This notice explains how SOMA processes personal data for the pilot.

SOMA is intended only for users aged 18 or over.

## 2. Data categories

Depending on features used, SOMA may process:

- account/authentication data;
- profile data and photographs;
- selected university/gym/community context;
- 18+ declaration evidence;
- dating preferences and dating-consent records;
- Chance/Spot metadata;
- Spotted posts, comments, likes, romantic initiatives and internal author identifiers;
- ORA place declarations and technical quota metadata;
- Match/Chat content and metadata;
- reports, moderation and safety evidence;
- technical logs, security events and pseudonymous analytics;
- rights-request and support records.

Spotted is anonymous **to other users**, not to SOMA. Internal identity may be retained where necessary for safety, moderation, abuse prevention and legal obligations.

ORA records a **user declaration of presence**. It does not certify physical presence and does not require mandatory GPS.

## 3. Special-category data

Dating preferences may in some cases reveal or permit inference about sexual life or sexual orientation.

Where Article 9 GDPR applies, SOMA uses a conservative model based on:

- Article 6(1)(a) GDPR; and
- Article 9(2)(a) GDPR explicit consent.

Legitimate interest is not used as a substitute for Article 9 where special-category processing applies.

## 4. Legal bases

Depending on purpose, SOMA may rely on:

- Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for processing objectively necessary to provide requested service functions;
- Article 6(1)(a) GDPR for consent-based processing;
- Article 9(2)(a) GDPR for explicit consent where special-category data are involved;
- Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, after balancing, for security, anti-abuse, moderation, system protection and minimized technical analytics;
- Article 6(1)(c) GDPR where a specific legal obligation applies.

There is no generic “privacy consent”.

## 5. Dating consent

Dating consent is:

- separate from Terms;
- explicit;
- specific;
- unpreselected;
- versioned;
- demonstrable;
- revocable.

A real **Non ora** option must be available.

The Privacy Policy is delivered as information and must not be presented as a consent checkbox.

## 6. Withdrawal

The in-app path should be:

**Profilo → Privacy e Dating → Preferenze dating**

After withdrawal:

- new consent-based dating processing stops;
- affected preferences are deleted or made unusable;
- no fallback to `ALL`;
- no implicit reconstruction of prior preferences;
- prior lawful processing is not invalidated.

Existing Match/Chat may continue only if they no longer depend on revoked preferences and are supported by the applicable legal basis.

## 7. Age assurance

The pilot uses **risk-based / progressive age assurance**.

Ordinary onboarding uses a mandatory, unpreselected 18+ declaration.

If reasonable doubts arise, SOMA may:

- restrict relevant features temporarily;
- review the account;
- request a proportionate additional age check;
- allow challenge/review.

The pilot does not require universal document upload, selfie age estimation or KYC for all users.

Google auth, QR access and membership in a university/gym/community do not prove age.

## 8. Chance

Current source of truth:

- up to 4 new Chance per day;
- release times 10:00, 14:00, 18:00, 21:30 Europe/Rome;
- reciprocal introduction;
- one person at a time;
- 24-hour expiry;
- one Spot per Chance;
- no general gallery.

Selection may consider compatibility, reciprocity, exposure fairness, novelty, diversity and safety.

No attractiveness/desirability score is used.

## 9. Spotted

Current source of truth:

- board per context;
- post max 280 characters;
- posts/comments visible 24h;
- likes/comments anonymous to peers;
- romantic initiative max 1 per user per day globally;
- romantic request pending max 7 days;
- unordered-pair guard 30 days after decline/expiry.

Internal author identity is retained only as needed for legitimate operational/safety/legal purposes.

## 10. ORA

Current source of truth:

- manual Place or QR;
- no mandatory GPS;
- one live Place at a time;
- max 4h;
- max 3 distinct Places/day;
- max 1 new ORA Spot per Place/day.

Do not describe ORA as verified physical location.

## 11. Photos

The pilot may validate format, dimensions, security and storage.

The pilot does not use:

- facial recognition;
- biometric templates;
- sensitive inference from photos.

## 12. Providers

Current providers may include:

- Supabase;
- Vercel;
- Resend;
- Google OAuth / Google services;
- the support/privacy mailbox.

The mailbox operating model remains a pre-pilot gate.

Universities, gyms and communities do not automatically receive individual user data merely because their name is used as context in SOMA.

SOMA does not sell personal data.

## 13. International transfers

Some providers or subprocessors may process data outside the EEA.

Where required, SOMA must assess and rely on appropriate mechanisms such as:

- adequacy decisions;
- EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable;
- Standard Contractual Clauses;
- supplementary measures where necessary.

An EU database region must not be described as meaning that all processing remains in the EU.

## 14. Retention — baseline

Key baselines include:

- Chance visible 24h;
- Chance technical metadata max 30d;
- Spotted visible 24h;
- expired unreported Spotted purge target ≤7d;
- romantic request pending max 7d;
- pair guard max 30d;
- ORA live max 4h;
- ORA anti-abuse metadata max 30d where needed;
- dating preferences while consent active, removal target ≤24h after withdrawal;
- pseudonymous analytics 90d;
- ordinary logs 14d;
- ordinary moderation 12 months;
- serious safety 24 months;
- moderation audit 24 months;
- incident evidence 24 months;
- minimum ban-evasion record up to 24 months;
- minimum rights-request evidence up to 5 years;
- direct backup baseline 7d, subject to provider-specific realities.

## 15. Inactive accounts

After 24 months of inactivity SOMA may schedule account deletion, with at least 30 days' warning where reasonably possible.

## 16. Account deletion

Deletion must occur without undue delay.

Target for active systems: ≤30 days, subject to documented exceptions such as legal obligations, safety, legal hold and backup rotation.

Deletion must not be falsely described as immediate universal erasure.

## 17. Backups

Backups must not be used to reintroduce data already deleted or consent already withdrawn.

After restore, deletion/revocation/suspension state must be replayed before normal service reopening.

## 18. Analytics and advertising

Pilot principles:

- no sale of personal data;
- no behavioral advertising;
- no ad SDK for behavioral profiling;
- minimized/pseudonymized analytics;
- no use of moderation data for romantic ranking.

## 19. Security

SOMA should use measures proportionate to risk, including:

- server-side authorization;
- RLS/access control;
- least privilege;
- ADMIN/MODERATOR separation;
- MFA for privileged users;
- audit;
- secret management;
- session revocation;
- upload controls;
- retention/cleanup;
- incident response.

## 20. Rights

Where applicable, users may exercise:

- access;
- rectification;
- erasure;
- restriction;
- objection;
- portability;
- consent withdrawal.

Requests may be submitted to somadatingapp@gmail.com.

Identity checks must be proportionate.

Third-party information, reporter identity and safety/anti-fraud information must be protected.

## 21. Complaints

Users may complain to the competent supervisory authority, including the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali where applicable.

## 22. Updates

Material changes to processing may require updated notice and, where legally required, a new consent event.

Changing this Privacy Policy does not replace a new consent where one is legally required.
